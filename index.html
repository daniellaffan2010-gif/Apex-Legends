Isometric wheel-to-wheel racing under the 2026 rules: lighter, narrower cars, active aero on the straights, and a manual override boost you have to earn back under braking. Twelve circuits, twenty-two cars, one championship.

Circuits are stylised interpretations — the corner sequences, elevation and colour palettes are built to read at an isometric angle rather than to survey-grade accuracy. Driver line-ups reflect the 2026 grid as announced; unconfirmed seats are filled with the most likely candidate.

The fitted setup applies to your car only, in every mode. Every slider trades one thing for another: downforce costs top speed, a long gearbox costs pull, forward brake bias costs stopping force, hard tyres cost grip. Clean laps are recorded automatically; pit and recovery laps and laps with cut corners are not.
